Original Platinum window graphics from pret/pokeplatinum, commit 1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20.
https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum/tree/1dc13dcea40c04ab53f1f607de46023dc409ef20/res/graphics/windows

20 message-box styles plus standard field/system windows. Pixel art and palettes are unmodified. PNG transparency metadata marks DS palette index zero as transparent.
Message frames retain their native 16-pixel left and 24-pixel right tile regions, as mapped by src/render_window.c. Existing GBA frames retain their native 8-pixel sides.
